  • Finalist, Fall 2026 BYU Economics Data Challenge

    Utah Water Crisis Case

    The Great Salt Lake is at a critically low level with potential damage to health levels in the Salt Lake Valley and areas downwind, destruction to bird populations, and reduction in associated industry and snow pack. We propose a shared responsibility model.

    Our model starts with a scarcity tax on water alongside a mandated curtailment. As returns from the scarcity tax diminish a mandate fine will help fund a lease-to-own program that allows the state to buy water rights from willing sellers. The fine is intended to increase willingness to sell. With additional levers and an allotment of $100M a year, our model gets the Great Salt Lake back to a healthy level by 2055.
