Utah Water Crisis Case

The Great Salt Lake sits well below the level its ecosystem needs, and almost all of the missing water is consumed upstream. This interactive model builds a $100M-a-year package of policy levers aimed at the 875 thousand acre-feet of added inflow the lake needs each year, and weighs what each lever costs and who supports it.

A visual simulation draws the basin block by block: snow on the Wasatch, rivers carrying it to the lake, and the shoreline moving as you change a lever or play the years forward to 2055.

Team of three. I designed and built the model.
